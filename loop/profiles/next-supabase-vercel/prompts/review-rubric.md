The scores are for UI items, calibrated against `DESIGN.md` and the accepted mocks in
     `design/mocks/accepted/` (open and screenshot them as your reference for an 8–9): **design
     quality** (a coherent whole in the project's design language), **originality** (the project's
     own voice, not template layouts or generic AI patterns), **craft** (hierarchy, spacing,
     alignment, contrast, states, 360 px without horizontal scroll), **functionality** (a first-time
     user finds the primary action and finishes the task).
